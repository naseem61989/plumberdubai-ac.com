What Water Filtration System Is Right for Your Dubai Home?

TDS levelshard waterRO membrane

Choosing the right water filtration system for your home starts with understanding what's actually in Dubai's tap water.

Municipal supply here consistently shows TDS levels between 200 and 500 mg/L, and hard water with elevated calcium and magnesium is the norm in most districts. For drinking water purity, a reverse osmosis system with an RO membrane is the most effective option. For broader protection — covering showers, appliances, and every tap — a whole-house filter makes more sense. Portable countertop filters and DIY water filtration system kits suit renters or anyone who can't modify plumbing. The best water filtration system for your kitchen depends on space, water quality goals, and budget. Our team assesses all three before making a recommendation.

02

Under-Sink and Reverse Osmosis (RO) Filter Installation

RO membranecarbon blocksediment filter

Under-sink water filter installation is the most requested service we handle, and an RO filter installation typically takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours from start to finish.

A standard reverse osmosis water filter installation includes a sediment filter stage to catch particles, a carbon block stage to remove chlorine and chloramines, the RO membrane itself (which strips dissolved solids down to 0.0001 microns), and a dedicated drinking water tap fitted through a small countertop hole. Adding a water filter to your kitchen sink requires roughly 40 cm of cabinet width and access to the cold supply line and drain. We also carry out alkaline water filter installation and install filtered water tap upgrades where the existing faucet is being replaced. Every install comes with a full water filter installation kit and a leak check before we leave.

03

Whole-House Water Filtration System Installation

point-of-entry filterwater softenerpre-filter housing

A whole-house water filter installation treats every litre entering your property through a single point-of-entry filter fitted on the main supply line — usually just after the stop valve.

Whole house water filtration system installation costs vary widely (covered in the cost section), but the plumbing scope is consistent: we fit a pre-filter housing for sediment removal, connect the main filter vessel, and install a bypass valve so you can service the system without cutting off supply. Properties with a water softener added alongside the filtration system need additional pipework for the brine line and drainage. Outdoor whole house water filter installation is common in Dubai villas where the main riser is accessible in a service cupboard or external wall cavity. We also handle iSpring whole house water filter installation and work with most branded pre-filter housing configurations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does water filtration system installation cost in Dubai?

▼

The cost of water filter installation in Dubai varies by system type. An under-sink reverse osmosis (RO) system typically costs AED 800–1,800 for the unit plus installation, with annual filter replacements running AED 150–400. A whole-house filtration system (point-of-entry) costs significantly more, usually AED 2,500–7,000+ depending on flow rate, number of filtration stages, and pipe complexity. Ongoing maintenance for whole-house systems averages AED 300–800 per year. Factors that affect the final price include water supply type, existing pipework condition, and brand of system chosen. Always request an itemized quote before proceeding.

How long does it take to install a whole house water filter in Dubai?

▼

A standard whole-house water filter installation in Dubai typically takes 3–5 hours for a straightforward setup. More complex installations involving older pipework, multiple filter stages, or bypass valve additions can take 6–8 hours. Water supply to the property is usually shut off for 1–3 hours during the core plumbing work. Under-sink RO systems are faster, generally completed in 1.5–2.5 hours with minimal water downtime. It is advisable to schedule installation on a day when water disruption is manageable.

Can a reverse osmosis system be installed under my kitchen sink in Dubai?

▼

Yes, under-sink RO installations are one of the most common water filtration requests in Dubai. A standard installation includes the multi-stage RO unit, a dedicated drinking water tap, a storage tank (typically 8–12 litres), and connection to the drain line. The system requires a cabinet space of roughly 40 cm wide by 45 cm deep. The installer will connect the feed line to the existing cold water supply under the sink and drill a small hole in the countertop for the dedicated faucet. No major plumbing modifications are usually needed. Installation is typically completed in under 3 hours.

What areas in Dubai do you cover for water filtration system installation, and is same-day service available?

▼

If you are searching for water filter installation near me in Dubai, most established plumbing providers cover all major areas including Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, JLT, Business Bay, Deira, Bur Dubai, Jumeirah, Al Quoz, Mirdif, Arabian Ranches, and Palm Jumeirah. Same-day or next-day scheduling is generally available for under-sink RO systems. Whole-house installations may require 1–2 days lead time for equipment sourcing. When contacting a provider, confirm their coverage for your specific community and request an on-site assessment, which is typically free of charge.

What is the difference between an RO under-sink system and a whole-house water filtration system?

▼

A reverse osmosis (RO) system is a point-of-use solution installed under a single tap, typically the kitchen sink. It filters water to a very high purity level (removing up to 99% of dissolved solids, chlorine, and heavy metals) but only at that one outlet. Flow rate is slow, around 0.2–0.5 litres per minute, and it produces some wastewater. A whole-house or point-of-entry system treats all water entering the property, covering every tap, shower, and appliance. It uses sediment and carbon filtration to improve water quality throughout the home but does not achieve the same purity level as RO. RO is best for drinking water; whole-house filtration is best for protecting appliances, skin, and hair from chlorine and sediment. Many Dubai residents combine both systems for comprehensive coverage.
