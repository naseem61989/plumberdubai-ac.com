01

Drain Unblocking and High-Pressure Water Jetting

Blocked drains slow everything down — kitchens back up, bathrooms flood, and the smell alone signals a deeper problem.

We clear obstructions using high-pressure water jetting, which blasts through grease, scale, and debris without damaging the pipe walls. This method works on everything from kitchen sink lines to main sewer runs. For ground-floor units and villas, we also service the floor cleanout access points to ensure the full drain stack is clear. Most standard unblocking jobs are completed within two hours.

02

Leak Detection and Pipe Repair in Dubai

Hidden leaks inside walls or under slabs can go undetected for weeks, quietly warping structures and inflating DEWA water bills.

We use acoustic detection and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact leak location before any surface is opened. Once found, we repair or replace the damaged pipe section using materials suited to Dubai's water pressure and mineral content. Copper, CPVC, and PPR pipes are all within scope. Repairs are pressure-tested on completion so there's no second visit needed.

03

Water Heater Installation and Servicing

Whether you're replacing a failing tank unit or upgrading to a continuous-flow water heater, correct installation is critical for both performance and safety.

We work with all major brands available in Dubai, sizing the unit to the property's actual hot-water demand rather than guessing. Anode rods, pressure relief valves, and thermostats are checked during every service call. For solar-assisted systems common in Dubai villas, we handle the booster element and backup connections as part of the same job.

04

Bathroom and Kitchen Plumbing Fit-Out

New fit-outs require more than connecting taps — the supply lines, waste runs, and venting must be positioned to Dubai Building Code standards from day one.

We rough-in for basins, showers, bathtubs, bidets, and kitchen sinks, then return to set the fixtures once tiling is complete. Every connection is sealed and tested before handover. We coordinate with contractors on timing to avoid delays, and we carry the most common fittings in the van so minor last-minute changes don't push the schedule.

05

Water Filtration System Installation in Dubai Homes

Dubai's mains water is safe but heavily treated, and the taste and hardness affect both daily use and appliance longevity.

A properly specified water filtration system home setup addresses sediment, chlorine, and dissolved solids at the point of entry or under the kitchen sink. We assess your water quality, recommend a filter grade matched to actual TDS readings, and install the system with dedicated supply lines. Post-installation, we show you how to monitor filter life and schedule the first cartridge replacement before we leave.

06

Sewage and Waste Line Services

Sewage backflow is one of the most disruptive plumbing failures a Dubai property can face, particularly in older apartment buildings where shared stacks become partially blocked over years.

We inspect waste lines using a drain camera, locate root intrusion or scale buildup, and clear it with water jetting or mechanical rodding depending on severity. Floor cleanout caps are replaced where corroded or missing. For properties experiencing repeated blockages, we map the waste network and recommend a long-term maintenance schedule to prevent recurrence.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire a plumber near me in Dubai?

▼

Plumbing service costs in Dubai typically range from AED 150 to AED 500 for standard jobs like unclogging drains or fixing minor leaks. More complex tasks such as pipe replacements or water heater installations can run between AED 500 and AED 2,000+. Costs vary based on the type of repair, materials required, time of day, and accessibility of the plumbing system. Always request a written quote before work begins.

How quickly can an emergency plumber in Dubai respond to a call?

▼

Most licensed emergency plumber Dubai services offer response times between 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on your location and time of day. Areas like Downtown Dubai, JLT, and Dubai Marina typically see faster response times due to higher service density. Emergency call-outs usually carry a premium charge of AED 100 to AED 300 on top of standard labor rates, especially outside regular business hours.

How long does it take to fix a leaking pipe in Dubai?

▼

The time needed to fix leaking pipe issues depends on the pipe's location and severity of the leak. A visible pipe repair in an accessible area typically takes 1 to 3 hours. If the leak is behind walls or under flooring, diagnosis and repair can extend to 4 to 8 hours due to detection work and surface restoration. Water damage assessment and drying may add additional time.

What plumbing issues are considered emergencies in Dubai?

▼

Plumbing emergencies in Dubai include burst pipes, complete loss of water supply, sewage backflow, gas-water heater leaks, and flooding from broken fixtures. These situations require immediate attention to prevent structural damage and health hazards. If you experience any of these, shut off the main water valve first, then contact a licensed emergency plumber. Dubai Municipality regulations require plumbers to hold a valid trade license, so verify credentials before hiring.

What factors affect plumbing repair costs in Dubai apartment buildings?

▼

Plumbing repair costs in Dubai apartments are influenced by building age, pipe material (copper, PVC, or galvanized steel), floor level, and whether the issue is within the unit or in shared infrastructure. Older buildings with galvanized pipes often require full section replacements, costing AED 800 to AED 3,000. Landlord versus tenant liability also affects who pays, governed by Dubai's Tenancy Law. Always check your tenancy contract and contact your building management company before hiring a plumber near me independently.
