How Do You Know You Have an Underground Water Leak?

water bill spikedamp patchlow pressure

An underground water leak rarely announces itself clearly — instead, it shows up as a pattern of small, easy-to-dismiss symptoms.

The most reliable early signal is a water bill spike of 20% or more with no change in household habits. Pair that with a damp patch appearing on a tiled floor, a wall base, or a section of garden that never fully dries out, and the case for an underground leak becomes strong. Low pressure that affects one or more fixtures — particularly on the ground floor — is another consistent indicator. If you hear water running when every tap and appliance is off, that sound almost always points to a pressurised pipe losing water somewhere below the surface. Catching this early typically saves thousands of dirhams in structural repair.

02

How Do You Find a Water Leak Underground Without Digging?

acoustic sensortracer gasthermal camera

Finding a water leak underground without excavation is straightforward when the right equipment is matched to the right conditions.

Our first step is always acoustic sensor placement: the device rests on the surface and listens for the specific vibration frequency that pressurised water makes when it escapes a pipe — usually between 100 and 2,000 Hz. Locating water leaks underground in sandy or compacted soil responds well to this method. For detecting leaks in underground water pipelines buried beneath thick concrete or screeded floors, we bring in tracer gas — a harmless hydrogen-nitrogen mix — which rises through the substrate to the surface at the exact breach point. A thermal camera runs alongside both methods, identifying temperature anomalies in soil or tiles that confirm the leak zone before any ground is opened.

03

Underground Leak Detection Equipment and Methods We Use

ground microphonecorrelatorleak noise logger

Detecting underground water accurately depends on calibrated equipment, not guesswork.

Our primary tool is a digital ground microphone — a professional underground water pipe leak detector that amplifies leak noise while filtering out traffic and mechanical interference. Alongside it, a correlator calculates the leak's position mathematically by comparing signal arrival times at two sensor points on the same pipe run; on a 20-metre pipe, this narrows the location to within 30 centimetres. For longer networks, a leak noise logger is left overnight to record pressure fluctuations without traffic noise masking the signal. Together, these make our setup one of the best underground water leak detector configurations available for Dubai's mixed-construction environment — useful on GI mains, CPVC domestic lines, and PVC irrigation runs alike.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does underground water leak detection cost in Dubai?

▼

Underground water leak detection in Dubai typically costs between AED 500 and AED 2,500, depending on the property size, accessibility, and detection method used. Acoustic listening devices are generally the most affordable option, while tracer gas detection — used for more complex or deep leaks — sits at the higher end of the range. Some leak detection companies in the UAE offer a no-fix-no-fee policy, meaning you only pay for the full service if the leak is successfully located. Hidden pipe leak repair costs are separate and vary based on pipe depth, material, and extent of damage. Always confirm whether the quoted price includes a written report before booking.

How do you find a water leak underground without digging up the garden?

▼

Professional technicians use three main non-invasive methods to detect an underground water leak without excavation. First, acoustic listening devices are placed on the ground surface to pick up the sound frequency of escaping water — this is the most common method for how to detect underground water leak in residential properties. Second, thermal imaging cameras identify temperature anomalies in soil or flooring caused by leaking water. Third, tracer gas detection involves injecting a harmless hydrogen-nitrogen gas mixture into the pipe; the gas escapes at the leak point and is detected at the surface using a sensitive probe. These methods can also locate a water leak under floor slabs and concrete without breaking ground until the exact location is confirmed.

How long does underground leak detection take in Dubai?

▼

A standard on-site underground leak detection inspection in Dubai typically takes between 1 and 4 hours, depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the pipe network. Smaller villas or apartments are usually completed within 1 to 2 hours. Larger commercial properties or cases requiring multiple detection methods — such as combining acoustic testing with tracer gas — may take closer to 3 to 4 hours. A formal inspection report indicating the suspected leak location is generally provided on the same day or within 24 hours. If hidden pipe leak repair is required, that is scheduled as a separate appointment after the report is reviewed.

What are the signs of an underground water leak in a Dubai property?

▼

Common indicators of an underground water leak include: a sudden or unexplained increase in your water bill without a change in usage habits; damp or wet patches appearing on walls, floors, or in the garden with no obvious cause; reduced water pressure throughout the property; the sound of running water when all taps and appliances are turned off; cracks appearing in flooring or walls due to soil movement caused by escaping water; and unusually lush or green patches of grass in one specific area of the garden. A water leak under floor slabs can also cause tiles to lift or grout to crack. If you notice two or more of these signs simultaneously, it is advisable to arrange a professional leak detection inspection rather than waiting, as underground leaks typically worsen over time and can cause structural damage.
