Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs of a water leak under the floor in a Dubai home?

▼

Common signs of a water leak under the floor include unexplained increases in your water bill, warm or damp spots on floor surfaces, the sound of running water when all taps are closed, mold or mildew odors at floor level, and visible cracks forming in tiles or flooring. In Dubai, where concrete slab construction is standard, these symptoms often indicate a pressurized pipe failure beneath the slab. If you notice two or more of these signs simultaneously, arrange a professional leak detection inspection within 24–48 hours to prevent structural damage.

How do you find a slab leak accurately without breaking the floor?

▼

Professional technicians use non-invasive methods to find a slab leak before any excavation begins. The most common techniques include electronic acoustic listening devices that detect water movement through concrete, thermal imaging cameras that identify temperature anomalies caused by leaking water, and tracer gas detection using inert hydrogen-nitrogen gas injected into the pipe. In Dubai, most reputable plumbing contractors complete the detection phase in 1–3 hours. This diagnostic step typically costs AED 200–600 and prevents unnecessary floor damage by pinpointing the exact leak location before any cutting begins.

How much does slab leak repair cost in Dubai?

▼

Slab leak repair costs in Dubai typically range from AED 1,500 to AED 8,000 depending on several factors: the depth and accessibility of the leaking pipe, the pipe material (copper, CPVC, or galvanized steel), the repair method chosen, and the extent of water damage already present. A localized spot repair with minimal concrete cutting usually starts around AED 1,500–3,000. Full pipe rerouting or epoxy pipe lining, which avoids breaking the slab entirely, can range from AED 3,500 to AED 8,000 or more for larger properties. Always request an itemized written quote after the detection phase.

How long does slab leak repair take in Dubai?

▼

The total time depends on the repair method. Leak detection alone takes 1–3 hours. A straightforward spot repair involving cutting, fixing, and patching the concrete slab typically takes 4–8 hours in a single day. Pipe rerouting through walls or ceilings, which avoids breaking the floor, can take 1–2 days. Full epoxy pipe lining for an entire system may take 2–3 days. Floor tile or surface restoration, if required, adds an additional 1–3 days after the plumbing work is complete. Most standard residential slab leak repairs in Dubai are resolved within one working day.

How do I find the best slab leak repair near me in Dubai, and what should I check before hiring?

▼

To find the best slab leak repair near me in Dubai, verify the following before hiring: confirm the contractor holds a valid Dubai Municipality or DEWA-approved plumbing license, ask whether they use electronic or thermal leak detection equipment rather than guesswork excavation, request references or verified Google reviews for slab leak work specifically, and ensure they provide a written scope of work with a fixed or capped price after the diagnostic phase. Avoid contractors who quote repairs without first conducting a formal leak detection. Response time matters too — for active leaks causing water damage, choose a company that offers same-day or emergency service, which most established Dubai plumbing firms provide within 2–4 hours.
