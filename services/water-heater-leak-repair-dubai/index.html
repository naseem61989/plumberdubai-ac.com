Why Is My Water Heater Leaking?

corroded tankloose fittingvalve failure

Most water heater leakage traces back to one of three culprits: a corroded tank, a loose fitting, or valve failure.

In Dubai, hard water speeds up sediment buildup inside the tank, which accelerates internal rusting — a corroded tank eventually cracks from the inside and no sealant fixes that reliably. Loose fittings at the inlet and outlet connections are very common after years of thermal cycling and are straightforward water tank leak repairs. Valve failure — particularly at the pressure relief valve or drain valve — accounts for a large share of drips that look alarming but are actually simple to resolve with the right parts on hand.

03

Is a Leaking Geyser Tap Something You Can Fix?

tap washervalve replacement

Yes — a leaking geyser tap is almost always repairable without touching the tank itself.

The most frequent fix involves replacing a worn tap washer, which costs very little and takes under 30 minutes. If the tap body is cracked or the internal seat is damaged, a full valve replacement is the cleaner solution and still far cheaper than replacing the unit. We carry standard tap washers and compatible valve assemblies in our service vehicles, so in most cases the job is completed on the first visit. A dripping geyser tap wastes around 15 litres of hot water per day — worth fixing promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does water heater leak repair cost in Dubai?

▼

The cost of water heater leak repair in Dubai depends on the source and severity of the leak. A faulty pressure relief valve or geyser tap repair typically costs AED 80–200 including parts. Fixing a loose pipe fitting or connection usually falls between AED 100–250. However, if you have a hot water tank leaking due to internal corrosion or a cracked tank body, full replacement is often required, which can cost AED 600–2,500+ depending on the unit's size and brand. A technician should inspect the leak before quoting, as misdiagnosing the source can lead to repeat failures.

Why is my water heater leaking from the bottom?

▼

A water heater leaking from the bottom is most commonly caused by internal tank corrosion. Over time, sediment builds up at the base of the tank and accelerates rusting from the inside out, eventually causing the steel lining to crack or fail. Once the tank itself is breached, no patch or sealant provides a reliable long-term fix. Other less severe causes include a leaking drain valve at the base or condensation, both of which are repairable. If the leak is traced to the tank body itself, replacement is the only safe and lasting solution. Continued use of a corroded tank also risks water damage and pressure buildup hazards.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired without replacement?

▼

Yes, many water heater leaks can be repaired without replacing the full unit — but it depends entirely on where the leak originates. Leaks from the pressure relief valve, inlet/outlet pipe fittings, drain valve, or geyser tap are almost always repairable at a reasonable cost. These components can be replaced or resealed within 1–3 hours. However, if the hot water tank itself is leaking due to internal corrosion or a fracture in the tank body, repair is not a viable option — the entire unit must be replaced. A qualified plumber will assess the leak source before recommending a repair or replacement.

Do you offer emergency water heater leak repair in Dubai?

▼

Same-day and emergency leak repair services are available across Dubai, including areas such as Dubai Marina, Deira, Jumeirah, Business Bay, and Al Barsha. If you are searching for an emergency plumber near me for a sudden water heater or geyser failure, response times typically range from 30 to 90 minutes depending on your location and time of day. Emergency call-out fees in Dubai generally add AED 50–150 on top of standard repair costs. It is advisable to shut off the water supply valve connected to the heater and turn off the power or gas supply while waiting for the technician to prevent further damage or safety risks.

What are the most common causes of a hot water tank leaking in Dubai?

▼

The most frequent causes of a hot water tank leaking in Dubai include: (1) Corrosion — Dubai's hard water accelerates sediment buildup and internal rusting, particularly in tanks older than 5–8 years; (2) High water pressure — pressure exceeding 80 PSI can stress tank joints and valves, leading to drips at fittings; (3) A faulty pressure relief valve — this safety component can start dripping when it wears out or when pressure inside the tank is consistently too high; (4) Loose or degraded pipe connections — these are common after years of thermal expansion and contraction; and (5) A failed drain valve. Leak repair Dubai specialists typically conduct a full diagnostic check covering all these points before beginning any repair work.
