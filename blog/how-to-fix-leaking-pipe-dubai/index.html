How to Fix a Leaking Pipe in Dubai: Step-by-Step Guide for Homeowners

By Best Plumbers Dubai
Posted on August 17, 2026 · 5 min read

A leaking pipe is one of the most stressful plumbing problems a homeowner can face, especially in Dubai where water damage can escalate quickly due to the combination of high water pressure, extreme heat, and aging building materials. Whether it is a slow drip under the kitchen sink or a more serious burst pipe behind a wall, knowing how to respond promptly can save you thousands of dirhams in repair costs. This guide walks you through exactly how to fix a leaking pipe in Dubai, step by step, so you can take control of the situation with confidence.

Dubai's climate and water supply conditions create unique challenges for residential plumbing. The high mineral content in the water supply accelerates pipe corrosion, while summer temperatures can cause pipes to expand and contract, weakening joints over time. Understanding these local factors is essential before you attempt any repair.

In this post, you will learn how to identify the source of a leak, gather the right tools, apply a temporary or permanent fix depending on the severity, and know when it is time to call a professional. Whether you are a seasoned DIY enthusiast or a first-time homeowner, this guide gives you the knowledge to handle a leaking pipe situation safely and effectively.

Step 1 – Identify the Source and Shut Off the Water Supply

Before you do anything else, locate the source of the leak. This sounds straightforward, but water has a habit of traveling along pipes before dripping, which means the visible wet spot may not be directly above the actual leak. Check under sinks, around toilet bases, near water heaters, and along exposed pipes in utility areas. If the leak is inside a wall, look for bubbling paint, damp patches, or a musty odor as tell-tale signs.

Once you have identified the general area, shut off the water supply immediately. Every home in Dubai should have a main shut-off valve, usually located near the water meter or in a utility cupboard. Turn it clockwise to close it. If the leak is isolated to one fixture, such as a bathroom sink, you can shut off the local valve beneath that fixture instead of cutting water to the entire property.

After shutting off the water, open the nearest tap to release any remaining pressure in the pipes. This step is critical — attempting to work on a pressurized pipe is both dangerous and ineffective. Place towels and a bucket under the leak area to catch residual water before you begin the repair process.

How to Fix a Leaking Pipe in Dubai: Choosing the Right Repair Method

The repair method you choose depends entirely on the type and severity of the leak. For small pinhole leaks or hairline cracks in copper or CPVC pipes — which are common in Dubai apartments — a pipe repair clamp or epoxy pipe repair compound is a reliable short-term solution. Pipe repair clamps are available at most hardware stores in areas like Al Quoz or Deira, and they can be fitted in minutes without specialized tools. Simply center the rubber gasket over the leak, place the clamp around it, and tighten the bolts firmly.

For leaking pipe joints, the most effective DIY fix is to drain the line completely, dry the joint thoroughly, and apply a waterproof epoxy putty. Knead the two-part putty together until it forms a uniform color, press it firmly around the joint, and allow it to cure for the time specified on the packaging — usually 30 to 60 minutes. This method works well on both metal and plastic pipes and can hold for several months under normal water pressure.

If the leak is on a threaded connection, the issue is often degraded thread seal tape (PTFE tape). Unscrew the fitting, remove the old tape, wrap three to five layers of fresh PTFE tape clockwise around the male threads, and reassemble the connection. This is one of the simplest and most effective repairs a homeowner can carry out independently. Always avoid overtightening, as this can crack plastic fittings — a particularly common problem in Dubai's older residential buildings.

When to Stop DIY and Call a Professional Plumber in Dubai

While the steps above are effective for minor leaks, there are clear situations where a professional plumber is the only safe option. If you notice water seeping through walls or ceilings, if multiple fixtures are affected simultaneously, or if you hear a hissing sound suggesting a pressurized burst, stop all DIY attempts and call a licensed plumber immediately. These signs often point to a mainline failure that requires specialist equipment and experience to repair correctly.

Dubai's building regulations also require that any significant plumbing work in apartments and villas be carried out by a licensed contractor. Attempting major repairs yourself without the proper permits could void your building insurance and create liability issues if the damage worsens. It is always worth checking your tenancy agreement as well — in many cases, the landlord or property management company is responsible for structural plumbing repairs.

Another important consideration is water damage remediation. Even after you fix the leak, moisture trapped inside walls or under flooring can lead to mold growth, which thrives in Dubai's humid summer conditions. A professional plumber will not only repair the pipe but can also advise on drying out the affected area properly, potentially saving you from a far more expensive problem down the line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common signs of a hidden leak include an unexplained increase in your DEWA water bill, damp or discolored patches on walls or ceilings, a persistent musty smell, and the sound of running water when all taps are turned off. If you suspect a hidden leak, shut off all water outlets and check whether your water meter continues to move — if it does, you almost certainly have a leak somewhere in the system.
PTFE (plumber's) tape is excellent for sealing threaded connections and preventing leaks at joints, but it is not designed to patch cracks or holes in the pipe body itself. For a permanent fix on a cracked or corroded pipe, the damaged section should be cut out and replaced. Plumber's tape is best used as part of a proper joint assembly rather than as a standalone repair solution.
The cost varies depending on the severity of the leak, the pipe material, and the location of the damage. Minor repairs such as fixing a leaking joint or replacing a tap washer typically range from AED 100 to AED 300. More complex repairs involving pipe replacement inside walls can range from AED 500 to AED 2,000 or more. Getting a written quote from a licensed plumber before work begins is always recommended.
