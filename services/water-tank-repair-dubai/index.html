Can a Cracked or Leaking Tank Be Patched Instead of Replaced?

epoxy patchrepair tapefiberglass compound

Yes — most cracked or leaking tanks can be fixed without full replacement, provided the damage is caught early enough.

Our technicians use food-grade epoxy patch compound on fiberglass and polyethylene tanks, pressing the material in layers and allowing it to cure for 4–8 hours before the tank is refilled. For surface weeps on plastic tanks, waterproof repair tape or a fiberglass compound kit provides a reliable short-term fix, while epoxy-based water tank repair material handles long-term structural sealing. Knowing how to repair a water tank correctly means cleaning the surface completely dry first — any moisture stops the patch from bonding. If a crack spans more than 30% of a panel, patching alone won't hold safely.

Repair vs Replace: Which Does Your Tank Need?

GRP tank lifespanwarrantytank age

Choosing between repair and replacement comes down to three things: the tank's age, the extent of structural damage, and whether a patch has already been attempted more than twice.

A GRP tank lifespan under normal Dubai conditions runs 15–25 years. If your tank is under 12 years old with an isolated crack, water tank repair services are almost always the smarter financial choice. Beyond 20 years, especially when wall sections feel brittle or UV-bleached, replacement becomes more cost-effective over the following 5-year period. A new GRP tank carries a manufacturer warranty of 10–15 years. We assess tank age, wall thickness, and pressure rating before recommending any course of action — we won't push replacement when a repair will hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does water tank repair cost in Dubai?

▼

Water tank repair costs in Dubai vary depending on the type and severity of the damage. A small crack patch using epoxy or fiberglass compound typically costs between AED 150–350. Replacing a faulty pressure valve or float valve usually runs AED 200–500 including parts and labor. Pump repair or replacement on a water tank system can range from AED 400–1,200 depending on pump size and brand. Full liner replacement or major structural repair on a rooftop tank may cost AED 800–2,500+. For accurate pricing, a technician needs to inspect the tank on-site, as tank size, material (fiberglass, polyethylene, concrete), and accessibility all affect the final cost. Searching for water tank repair near me in Dubai will surface local specialists who can provide same-day assessments.

Can a cracked water tank be repaired, or does it need to be replaced?

▼

Yes, most cracked water tanks can be repaired without full replacement. Small to medium cracks in fiberglass or polyethylene tanks are commonly fixed using food-grade epoxy resin, fiberglass patching compound, or specialized waterproof tape. The repair holds reliably when the surface is properly cleaned, dried, and the compound is allowed to fully cure — typically 4–8 hours before refilling. Replacement is the safer option when: the crack spans more than 30% of a panel, the tank wall has become structurally brittle from UV damage or age, there are multiple leak points, or the tank is more than 15–20 years old. A qualified technician performing water tank service in Dubai will assess whether a patch is structurally sound or whether continued patching is a short-term fix that makes replacement more cost-effective long-term.

How do you patch a leaking water tank?

▼

Here is the standard process used for water tank leak fixing by professional technicians in Dubai: 1) Shut off the water supply and drain the tank completely. 2) Identify all leak points — there are often multiple small cracks near one visible one. 3) Clean and dry the damaged area thoroughly; any moisture prevents the patch from bonding. 4) For fiberglass or plastic tanks, apply a food-grade epoxy compound or fiberglass repair kit in layers, pressing firmly over the crack. For minor surface weeps, waterproof repair tape can be used as a temporary fix. 5) Allow the patch to cure for the manufacturer-recommended time, usually 4–8 hours. 6) Refill the tank slowly and monitor for 30–60 minutes before restoring full supply. For concrete tanks, hydraulic cement or polymer-modified mortar is used instead of epoxy. DIY patching is possible for minor leaks, but professional repair is recommended to ensure the patch is food-safe and pressure-tested.

Who repairs water tanks in Dubai, and what should I look for in a service provider?

▼

Water tank repairs in Dubai are carried out by licensed plumbing and maintenance companies, as well as specialist water tank service providers. When choosing who repairs your water tank, look for these qualifications: a valid Dubai Municipality or RERA trade license, technicians certified in water hygiene and tank maintenance, use of food-grade, NSF-certified repair materials (critical for drinking water tanks), documented experience with your tank type — fiberglass, polyethylene, concrete, or steel — and transparent pricing with a written quote before work begins. A reputable water tank service in Dubai provider will also offer a post-repair water quality check and a short-term workmanship warranty, typically 3–12 months on the repair. Avoid companies that cannot provide a license number or refuse to give a written estimate.
