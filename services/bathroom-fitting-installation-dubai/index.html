How Much Does Bathroom Fitting Cost in Dubai?

sanitarywaretiling packagefit-out quote

Bathroom fitting cost in Dubai depends heavily on scope.

A single fixture swap — toilet, basin, or shower unit — runs AED 300–800 in labour. A partial upgrade covering new sanitaryware and a tiling package typically lands between AED 2,500 and AED 6,000. A full fit-out quote — demolition, waterproofing, tiling, and all fixtures supplied and fitted — generally starts at AED 8,000 and climbs to AED 25,000+ for larger or premium bathrooms. The cost of installing a new shower or standing shower averages AED 1,200–3,500 depending on glass type, and the cost of installing a new bathtub ranges from AED 900 to AED 4,000 installed. Bathroom fittings prices in the UAE rise with material grade, so ask your fitter to separate supply costs from labour on any quote.

How Do I Install a Bathroom? (Step-by-Step)

demolitionplumbing rough-infinishing snag list

Fitting a new bathroom follows a fixed sequence that professionals never shortcut.

Step 1 is demolition: tiles are chipped off, old fixtures disconnected, and all debris cleared — typically a full day for a standard Dubai apartment bathroom. Step 2 is plumbing rough-in: supply and drain pipes are moved to match the new layout and pressure-tested before walls close up. Step 3 is waterproofing: a membrane goes onto all wet surfaces and cures for at least 24 hours. Step 4 is tiling: floors first, then walls, grouted and sealed. Step 5 is fixture installation — toilet, basin, bath, and shower fittings are all connected and tested. Step 6 is the finishing snag list: silicone, accessories, mirror, and a final leak check before handover.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does bathroom fitting cost in Dubai?

▼

Bathroom fitting costs in Dubai vary significantly based on scope. A single fixture swap — such as replacing a toilet, basin, or shower unit — typically costs AED 300–800 including labour. A partial bathroom upgrade (new sanitaryware, taps, and accessories without structural changes) usually falls between AED 2,500–6,000. A full bathroom fit-out covering demolition, waterproofing, tiling, plumbing, sanitaryware, and finishing generally starts from AED 8,000 and can reach AED 25,000+ depending on the size of the bathroom, quality of materials chosen, and complexity of the layout. Bathroom fitting services in Dubai may also charge separately for tile supply, so always confirm whether quotes are supply-only or supply-and-fit.

How long does it take to fit a new bathroom in Dubai, day by day?

▼

A full bathroom renovation in Dubai typically takes 7–12 working days from strip-out to completion. Here is a realistic day-by-day breakdown: Days 1–2 cover demolition — removing old tiles, fixtures, and fittings. Days 3–4 involve rough plumbing and electrical repositioning if required. Day 5 is for waterproofing the wet areas, which must cure for 24 hours before tiling begins. Days 6–8 cover wall and floor tiling, including grouting and drying time. Days 9–10 are for installing sanitaryware, a bathroom plumber Dubai will fit the toilet, basin, bath, and shower fixtures. Days 11–12 handle finishing touches — siliconing, accessory fitting, and a final inspection. Smaller jobs such as a single fixture replacement can be completed in 2–4 hours.

What does a full bathroom fit-out include in Dubai?

▼

A full bathroom fit-out through professional bathroom fitting services in Dubai typically includes the following: demolition and removal of existing fittings, waterproofing of walls and floors to UAE building standards, supply and installation of wall and floor tiles, installation of sanitaryware (toilet, basin, bathtub or shower tray), shower enclosure or screen fitting, installation of taps, mixers, and showerheads, connection of all waste and supply pipework by a qualified bathroom plumber Dubai, fitting of mirrors, towel rails, and accessories, silicone sealing around all wet joints, and a final pressure and leak test. Electrical work such as extractor fans or lighting is sometimes included depending on the contractor, so confirm this before signing a quote. Material costs for tiles and sanitaryware are usually quoted separately unless stated otherwise.

How is a bathroom installed step by step?

▼

A professional bathroom installation in Dubai follows this sequence. First, the existing bathroom is stripped out — tiles are removed, old fixtures are disconnected, and waste is cleared. Second, the plumber assesses and reroutes supply and drain pipework to suit the new layout. Third, walls and floors are prepared and a waterproof membrane is applied to all wet areas, particularly around the shower zone and bath. Fourth, tiling is carried out on floors first, then walls, followed by grouting and sealing. Fifth, sanitaryware is installed — the toilet is set and connected, the basin and vanity unit are fitted, and the bath or shower tray is positioned and plumbed in. Sixth, taps, mixers, shower systems, and waste fittings are connected and tested. Finally, accessories are fixed, silicone is applied at all junctions, and a full functional check is completed before handover.

Commonly covered areas include Dubai Marina, JBR, Jumeirah, Al Barsha, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Deira, Bur Dubai, Mirdif, Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills, Al Quoz, and Palm Jumeirah, among others.
