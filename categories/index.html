Leak Detection and Pipe Repairs in Dubai

Hidden leaks waste hundreds of litres before they're visible, and Dubai's concrete slab construction makes tracing them tricky without the right equipment.

Our team uses acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging to pinpoint the exact break, so we only open the wall or floor where the pipe actually failed. Most visible pipe repairs finish in under two hours. Concealed leaks behind tiles or within slabs typically take four to six hours including surface restoration. We use UAE-rated CPVC and copper fittings throughout, and every repair carries a 90-day workmanship guarantee.

02

Drain Cleaning and Blocked Drain Services

Slow drains and full blockages are among the most common calls we receive across Dubai apartments and villas.

Grease build-up, soap scale, and foreign objects account for the majority of cases. For standard blockages we use motorised drain snakes; for stubborn or recurring clogs we deploy high-pressure water jetting, which scours the pipe wall clean rather than just pushing the obstruction further down. Kitchen drains and bathroom waste stacks respond well to jetting, and we always inspect the floor cleanout access point to confirm the line is fully clear before closing the job.

03

Water Heater Installation and Repair

Dubai's hard water accelerates sediment build-up inside storage heaters, reducing efficiency and shortening the unit's life.

We service and install all major brands — Ariston, Rheem, Daal — and handle both electric and gas water heaters under Dubai Municipality safety standards. A standard element or thermostat replacement takes roughly 45 minutes. Full unit replacements, including safe disposal of the old heater and correct pressure valve installation, are typically completed within three hours. We also advise on sizing: a 100-litre unit suits most two-bedroom apartments, while larger villas generally need 150 litres or more.

04

Bathroom and Kitchen Plumbing Fitouts

Whether you're renovating a single bathroom or fitting out a full commercial kitchen, the quality of the rough-in plumbing determines how well everything performs for the next decade.

We supply and install mixers, sinks, toilets, bidets, and waste systems from brands stocked locally in Dubai. Our team reads architect drawings and coordinates with tiling contractors so pipe outlets land exactly where fixtures require them. We also handle grease trap installation for commercial kitchens, which Dubai Municipality mandates for food-service premises. Most single-bathroom fitouts are completed within one working day.

05

Water Filtration and Softener Systems

Dubai's tap water is safe to drink but carries high mineral content that affects taste and causes limescale inside appliances.

A properly sized water filtration system home installation removes chlorine, sediment, and excess minerals at the point of entry or under the sink. We install whole-house softeners, reverse osmosis units, and inline carbon filters. System selection depends on your building's supply pressure, household size, and the appliances you want to protect. After installation we test output TDS levels and set a service reminder — most filters need a cartridge change every six to twelve months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire a plumber near me in Dubai?

▼

Plumbing service costs in Dubai typically range from AED 150 to AED 500 for standard jobs like unclogging drains or fixing minor leaks. More complex tasks such as pipe replacements or water heater installations can run between AED 500 and AED 2,000+. Costs vary based on the type of repair, materials required, time of day, and accessibility of the plumbing system. Always request a written quote before work begins.

How quickly can an emergency plumber in Dubai respond to a call?

▼

Most licensed emergency plumber Dubai services offer response times between 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on your location and time of day. Areas like Downtown Dubai, JLT, and Dubai Marina typically see faster response times due to higher service density. Emergency call-outs usually carry a premium charge of AED 100 to AED 300 on top of standard labor rates, especially outside regular business hours.

How long does it take to fix a leaking pipe in Dubai?

▼

The time needed to fix leaking pipe issues depends on the pipe's location and severity of the leak. A visible pipe repair in an accessible area typically takes 1 to 3 hours. If the leak is behind walls or under flooring, diagnosis and repair can extend to 4 to 8 hours due to detection work and surface restoration. Water damage assessment and drying may add additional time.

What plumbing issues are considered emergencies in Dubai?

▼

Plumbing emergencies in Dubai include burst pipes, complete loss of water supply, sewage backflow, gas-water heater leaks, and flooding from broken fixtures. These situations require immediate attention to prevent structural damage and health hazards. If you experience any of these, shut off the main water valve first, then contact a licensed emergency plumber. Dubai Municipality regulations require plumbers to hold a valid trade license, so verify credentials before hiring.

What factors affect plumbing repair costs in Dubai apartment buildings?

▼

Plumbing repair costs in Dubai apartments are influenced by building age, pipe material (copper, PVC, or galvanized steel), floor level, and whether the issue is within the unit or in shared infrastructure. Older buildings with galvanized pipes often require full section replacements, costing AED 800 to AED 3,000. Landlord versus tenant liability also affects who pays, governed by Dubai's Tenancy Law. Always check your tenancy contract and contact your building management company before hiring a plumber near me independently.
