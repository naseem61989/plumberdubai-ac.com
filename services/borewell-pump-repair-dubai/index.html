Signs Your Borewell Pump Needs Repair

motor burnoutlow discharge

Your borewell pump is telling you something is wrong long before it stops entirely — you just need to know what to look for.

The clearest indicators are low discharge pressure at the tap and a motor that runs continuously without moving enough water. Motor burnout often announces itself through a burning smell near the control panel or a breaker that trips every few hours. Sputtering air mixed into the flow, discoloured or gritty water, and unusual humming all point to a well pump service call. Our well water pump service technicians see these patterns daily and can confirm the fault within the first 20 minutes on-site.

Borewell Pump Removal and Reinstallation

casing pipepump column

Borewell pump removal is a precision job — rushing it damages the casing pipe or snaps the pump column, turning a repair into a far costlier recovery operation.

Our crew uses purpose-built tripod rigs and safety clamps to lift the pump column in controlled stages, typically taking 45–90 minutes for borewells up to 100 metres deep. Once the pump is on the surface, we inspect the casing pipe for cracks, scale buildup, and corrosion before any components are touched. Reinstallation follows the same careful staging, with every joint checked and torqued to specification. A flow and pressure test after lowering confirms the pump is seated and performing correctly before we pack up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does borewell pump repair cost in Dubai?

▼

Borewell pump repair costs in Dubai typically range from AED 300 to AED 1,500 for standard repairs, depending on the depth of the borewell and the pump's horsepower rating. Shallow borewells (up to 50 metres) with 0.5–1 HP pumps are on the lower end, while deeper borewells (100+ metres) with 2–5 HP submersible pumps can push costs higher, especially if motor rewinding or impeller replacement is required. Water pump repair Dubai pricing also factors in spare parts availability and access difficulty. A full pump extraction, component overhaul, and reinstallation typically costs AED 800 to AED 2,500 inclusive of labour.

How do I know if my borewell pump needs repair or replacement?

▼

Common warning signs that your borewell pump not working correctly include: significantly reduced or no water flow despite the pump running, unusual grinding or humming noises from the motor, frequent circuit breaker trips, air sputtering from taps, or visibly discoloured water. If the pump is under 7 years old and symptoms are limited to one or two issues, repair is usually viable. If the pump is older than 10 years, draws excessive current, or has a burnt-out motor with corroded casing, full replacement is typically more cost-effective. A technician can confirm this with a resistance and current draw test on-site.

Is borewell pump repair available near me today in Dubai?

▼

Most professional plumbing services covering water pump repair Dubai operate across key areas including Deira, Bur Dubai, Jumeirah, Al Quoz, Mirdif, Dubai Silicon Oasis, and Dubai Investment Park. Same-day callout is generally available for borewell pump repair near me searches, with technicians typically able to reach most Dubai locations within 2 to 4 hours of booking. For emergency situations where there is a complete loss of water supply, priority same-day slots are usually offered, though availability can vary on public holidays.

How long does a borewell pump service or repair take?

▼

A standard borewell pump service typically takes 3 to 6 hours in total. The breakdown is roughly as follows: pump extraction from the borewell takes 45 to 90 minutes depending on depth and pipe configuration, on-site diagnosis and component repair or replacement takes 1 to 2 hours, and reinstallation plus pressure testing takes another 1 to 2 hours. If parts need to be sourced externally, such as a replacement motor or control panel, the job may extend to a second visit or take up to 24 hours. Rewinding a burnt motor, if sent off-site, can add 1 to 2 business days.

What are the most common causes of a borewell pump not working in Dubai?

▼

The most frequent causes of a borewell pump not working in Dubai include: voltage fluctuations or single-phasing from the power supply, which can burn out the motor winding; sand or sediment ingestion that damages impellers and seals; dry running due to low water table levels, particularly during summer months; corroded or cracked drop pipes causing loss of pressure; and failed capacitors in single-phase motors. Dubai's high mineral content in groundwater also accelerates scaling on pump components. Regular servicing every 12 to 18 months is generally recommended to prevent sudden failures and extend the pump's operational life.
